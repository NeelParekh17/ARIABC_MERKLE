  ---
  YCSB: Overhead Breakdown (det vs pg)

  Gate slot time — derived from det YCSB saturation TPS: gate processes txns
  serially, one at a time. At t=8-16, workload_overall_ms is flat:
  2047→2072→2036→2082ms (<2% spread). That flat ceiling = 20,000 txns ÷ 9769 TPS
   → gate slot = 102µs/txn.

  ┌─────────┬─────────┬────────┬─────────────┐
  │ threads │ det TPS │ pg TPS │   det/pg    │
  ├─────────┼─────────┼────────┼─────────────┤

  ├─────────┼─────────┼────────┼─────────────┤
  │ 16      │ 9605    │ 11153  │ 86.1%       │
  └─────────┴─────────┴────────┴─────────────┘

  t=1: det is 27% faster than pg. Reason: pg has 270 reconnects per run (23505
  unique-key violations on INSERT of existing keys). Reconnect cost = (6140ms −
  4846ms) / 270 = 4.8ms per reconnect. Total penalty = 1294ms. Without
  reconnects, pg t=1 would be ~4124 TPS ≈ det's 4127 TPS — they're equal at t=1
  (no gate queue at 1 thread, det overhead ≈ pg reconnect overhead).

  t=4-16: det 81-88% of pg. The serial gate is the entire gap. pg commits
  concurrently (MVCC, no serialization barrier). Det commits one-by-one through
  the gate. At saturation, 100% of elapsed time is consumed by gate slots: 9769
  TPS × 102µs/slot = 1.00s/s — every second is fully serial.

  ---
  Why det YCSB stops scaling at t=8

  The gate is a pure Amdahl serial section with serial fraction = 1.0 (all
  throughput flows through it).

  From summary.csv, det YCSB workload_overall_ms:
  t=6:  2094ms  (9551 TPS)  ← approaching saturation
  t=8:  2047ms  (9769 TPS)  ← gate limit hit
  t=10: 2072ms  (9629 TPS)  ← flat
  t=12: 2036ms  (9806 TPS)  ← flat
  t=16: 2082ms  (9605 TPS)  ← flat

  Adding threads deepens the gate queue but cannot reduce wall time below 20,000
   × 102µs = 2040ms. Every additional thread beyond 8 simply adds one more txn
  to the queue waiting for the same serial bottleneck.

  ---
  Why det YCSBTX drops after t=10 (exact derivation)

  The YCSBTX workload has 3800 DELETE→INSERT same-key pairs. The workload
  re-uses keys: DELETE(key K) at position d, INSERT(key K) at position d+dist.
  Pair distances are tightly clustered: dist=11 has 949 pairs, dist=10 has 613,
  dist=12 has 730.

  Blocking condition (derived exactly):

  With N threads, worker i handles workload positions i, N+i, 2N+i, ... The
  INSERT at position d+dist is worker (d+dist)%N's floor((d+dist)/N)-th
  operation. Its previous operation was at position d+dist−N. That previous txn
  gets TID ≈ d+dist−N.

  In det mode, INSERT's body starts after its prev TID commits. At that moment,
  last_committed = d+dist−N. For DELETE (TID ≈ d) to already be committed, need
  d+dist−N ≥ d, i.e., dist ≥ N. If dist < N, DELETE hasn't committed yet →
  INSERT body finds uncommitted xmax → heap lock wait.

  Blocked pairs by thread count (from full pair distance analysis of 3800
  pairs):

  ┌─────┬─────────────────────┬────────────┬─────────┬──────────────┐
  │  N  │ Pairs with dist < N │ % blocked  │ det TPS │ drop vs peak │
  ├─────┼─────────────────────┼────────────┼─────────┼──────────────┤
  │ 8   │ 14 (0.4%)           │ negligible │ 8501    │ baseline     │
  ├─────┼─────────────────────┼────────────┼─────────┼──────────────┤
  │ 10  │ 386 (10.2%)         │ mild       │ 8733    │ +2.7%        │
  ├─────┼─────────────────────┼────────────┼─────────┼──────────────┤

  The critical jump is N=10→12: blocked pairs explode from 386 (10%) to 1948

  Cascade amplification (why SELECT/DELETE also slow down):

  INSERT(txid=I) stuck waiting for DELETE(txid=D) also blocks all txns with txid
   > I — they can't pass through the gate until INSERT commits. Measured:

  Periodic 22-25ms spikes appear in SELECT and DELETE timing (seen in workload
  logs) — those are gate stall events when a cascade of blocked INSERTs freezes
  the queue, and downstream txns wait ~20ms for the chain to resolve.

  Why pg YCSBTX doesn't drop:

  pg has no serial gate. DELETE commits in standard PostgreSQL MVCC time
  (µs-to-ms, concurrent with other txns). By the time INSERT at position d+dist
  executes (11 positions later = many ms of real wall clock), DELETE has LONG
  since committed. No uncommitted xmax → INSERT proceeds without waiting. pg
  YCSBTX: 12,072→11,953→11,985 TPS from t=8→12→16 (flat, <0.5% variation).
